Face Swapping

Facial Identity Swapping through Latent Space Manipulation using Knowledge Distillation

A SimSwap-based face-swapping pipeline decomposed into encoder, AdaIN bottleneck, and decoder via knowledge distillation. Covers the full pipeline (SCRFD detection, ArcFace identity, AdaIN injection, BiSeNet parsing), all 7 loss functions with code, training convergence, and a 42% model size reduction from 210 MB to 122 MB.

Crowd Counting

Re-implementing APGCC: Auxiliary Point Guidance for Crowd Counting

APGCC tackles a core instability in point-based crowd counting: the assignment between predicted and ground-truth points shifts wildly across training epochs. This re-implementation walks through their auxiliary-point matcher, the dual-branch architecture, and the confidence-weighted loss that stabilises training.

Image Restoration

Histoformer: Histogram Self-Attention for All-Weather Image Restoration

Histoformer replaces standard self-attention with a histogram-based mechanism that captures the intensity distribution of degraded images. This article explains the architecture and re-implements the model for restoring images affected by rain, haze, and snow.
